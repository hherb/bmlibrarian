Welcome to BMLibrarian

Your own library of medical research, on this computer, searchable by meaning.

  1. We set up a private database and the AI that reads the papers, all on this Mac.
  2. You choose a topic; we download peer-reviewed, open-access full texts on it.
  3. You search them by meaning, as you would ask a colleague.

No administrator password needed. Nothing leaves this computer except the downloads.

Where your library lives

Your papers and their database go into one folder in your home folder.
